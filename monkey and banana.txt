Start.
Initialize the positions of the monkey, stool, stick, and banana.
Set on_stool = False.
Set has_stick = False.
Set has_banana = False.
If the monkey is not at the stool, move the monkey to the stool.
If the monkey is at the stick, pick up the stick.
If the stool is not under the banana, push the stool under the banana.
Climb onto the stool.
If the monkey is on the stool and has the stick, get the banana.
Display "Goal Achieved! Monkey got the banana."
Stop.
