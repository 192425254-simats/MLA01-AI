Start.
Define the known symptoms as facts.
Define rules connecting diseases with their symptoms.
Select a disease as the goal.
Check whether the goal is already a known fact.
If not, find a rule that can prove the goal.
Check each condition required by that rule.
Recursively work backward until the required conditions are found as facts.
If all conditions are satisfied, conclude that the disease is possible.
Otherwise, reject the goal.
Display the diagnosis.
Stop.
